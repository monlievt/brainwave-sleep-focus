Privacy Policy

Binaural Beats — Sleep & Focus
Last updated: August 16, 2026 Publisher: Monliev Labs

Short version: This app is designed to work completely offline. We do not collect your personal information, we do not send your usage data to our servers, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Overview

Binaural Beats — Sleep & Focus ("the App", "we", "our") is a brainwave entrainment app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the App. The App works entirely without user registration or logins.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Anonymous Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the App. This data includes app open/close events, which categories and presets are most used, and session duration. This data is completely anonymized and cannot be linked to you as an individual.

Crash Reporting: We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect technical diagnostic crash reports when the App encounters an unexpected error. Crash reports include device model, OS version, and stack traces, with no personal identification details.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact information.
  • Your physical location.
  • Your audio recordings or microphone input (the App does not request microphone permissions).
  • Your sleep patterns, biometric data, or health records.
  • Payment information (all transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play Billing).

3. Data Storage & Retention

All App settings, configurations, and user preferences (such as volume levels, selected categories, active timers, and dark mode toggles) are stored locally on your device only, using Android's secure local storage (SharedPreferences and DataStore). This data is:

  • Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Automatically deleted by the Android system when you uninstall the App.
  • Under your full control (you can wipe local preferences at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Brainwave > Storage > Clear Data).

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following Google Play Services to facilitate core functionalities. Each service operates under its own Google Privacy Policy:

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Google Play Billing Processes secure in-app purchases for premium unlocking. Google Privacy Policy
Google AdMob Displays mobile banner, native, and rewarded video ads. Google Privacy Policy
Firebase Services (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) Collects anonymous usage metrics, app crash logs, and facilitates push notifications. Google Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@monliev.com and we will take immediate steps to delete any trace of such information.

6. Health Information Disclaimer

This App provides audio content for relaxation, focus, and sleep support. It is not a medical device and does not collect, process, or transmit any health or biometric data.

The App does not monitor your heart rate, sleep stages, or any biometric signals; it does not diagnose or treat any medical condition; and it stores no health-related information about you.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data under the GDPR (EU/EEA) or CCPA (California). Since we do not transmit or store any personal data on our servers, these requirements are naturally met. For any requests or inquiries, you may contact us at support@monliev.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect all data processed by the App. Since your configurations stay exclusively on your device, the security surface remains small and secure. We recommend keeping your Android OS up to date for the best security features.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
